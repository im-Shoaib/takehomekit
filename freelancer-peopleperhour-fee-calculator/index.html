Freelancer.com fees

Freelancer.com keeps it simple. On fixed-price projects, the fee is 10% or $5, whichever is greater, charged when you accept the project. On hourly projects it's a straight 10% of each payment. Contests work like fixed-price projects: 10% or $5.

The $5 minimum matters on small jobs. A $30 project costs $5 in fees — that's 16.7%, not 10%. Above $50, the 10% rate takes over.

Clients pay their own fee on top: 3% or $3 on fixed-price projects, and 3% on hourly payments. It doesn't come out of your earnings.

Fixed price: earnings = amount − max(10% × amount, $5)

PeoplePerHour fees

PeoplePerHour uses a tiered fee based on how much you've billed each client over time. The more work you do for the same client, the lower your fee:

Lifetime billing with a clientService fee
Up to £250 ($350 / €300)20%
£250 to £5,000 ($7,000 / €6,000)7.5%
Above £5,0003.5%

There's also a minimum fee of about £1 per invoice, and freelancers in the UK and EU pay VAT on the fee. Sources disagree on the exact thresholds, so the tiers are editable above — check your PeoplePerHour account for the numbers that apply to you.

Example: £400 invoice to a new client

The first £250 is charged at 20% (£50) and the next £150 at 7.5% (£11.25). Total fee: £61.25, an effective 15.3%. You earn £338.75. Bill the same client another £400 later and the whole invoice falls in the 7.5% tier.

Which platform is cheaper?

  • Small one-off jobs: Freelancer.com's 10% usually beats PeoplePerHour's 20% first tier — unless the $5 minimum kicks in.
  • Long-term clients: PeoplePerHour wins. Once you pass the first tier, 7.5% and then 3.5% are among the lowest marketplace fees around.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Freelancer.com take from freelancers?

10% of fixed-price projects or $5, whichever is greater, and 10% of hourly payments.

Why was I charged $5 on a small Freelancer.com project?

Fixed-price projects have a $5 minimum fee. On any project under $50, $5 is more than 10%, so the minimum applies.

What are PeoplePerHour's fees?

A service fee that drops as your lifetime billing with each client grows: 20% on the first tier, then 7.5%, then 3.5%. A small minimum fee applies per invoice.

Does PeoplePerHour's fee reset for each project?

No. It's based on your total billing with the same client across all projects. A new client starts again at the first tier.

Do I pay VAT on PeoplePerHour fees?

If you're based in the UK or EU, VAT is usually added to the service fee. Tick the VAT box to include it.

Fiverr gig image size

Fiverr recommends gig gallery images of 1280 × 769 pixels at 72 DPI. The minimum is 712 × 430 px and the maximum is 4000 × 2416 px. All of these share the same shape — roughly 5:3 — so an image made at any of them displays without stretching.

Why your gig image looks cropped or blurry

  • Wrong shape. A square or tall image gets cropped in search results. Resizing to 1280 × 769 here means you choose the crop, not Fiverr.
  • Too small. If the original is smaller than the target size, it has to be enlarged and will look soft. The tool warns you when that happens.
  • Text near the edges. Fiverr's search cards show a slightly trimmed version of your image. Keep text and faces away from the outer edges.

Tips for a gig image that gets clicks

  • Show the result: a finished website, a logo on a mockup, a before-and-after.
  • Use a few large words at most. Thumbnails are small on mobile.
  • Don't add Fiverr badges, levels or ratings — Fiverr adds those itself and may remove images that include them.
  • Use JPG for photos and PNG for designs with sharp text.

Frequently asked questions

What size should a Fiverr gig image be?

1280 × 769 pixels is Fiverr's recommended size. The minimum is 712 × 430 and the maximum is 4000 × 2416.

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No. The image is processed entirely in your browser using the canvas, and the download is created on your device.

Why does my image look blurry after resizing?

The original is smaller than the target size, so it had to be enlarged. Start from a larger image or a design exported at 1280 × 769 or bigger.

Should I use JPG or PNG?

JPG for photos — the file is smaller. PNG for graphics with text or flat colours, which stay sharper.

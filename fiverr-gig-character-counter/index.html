    Fiverr gig character limits

    FieldLimit
    Gig title (including “I will”)80 characters
    Gig description1,200 characters (120 minimum)
    Search tagsUp to 5
    Seller profile description150–600 characters

    Characters include spaces and punctuation. Fiverr counts the “I will” at the start of your title, which is why the counter includes it.

    How to write a gig title that ranks

    • Lead with the keyword buyers type. “I will build a WordPress website” beats “I will be your expert web partner”.
    • Be specific. Name the platform, format or outcome: Shopify store, YouTube thumbnail, SEO blog post.
    • Don't stuff. One main keyword and one variation is enough. Titles that read like a list of tags look spammy to buyers.

    Writing the description

    Use the 1,200 characters to answer what a buyer is wondering: what exactly do I get, how fast, and why you? A structure that works well:

    1. One sentence on the result you deliver.
    2. A short bullet list of what's included.
    3. Why you — experience, speed, a specific niche.
    4. A clear next step: “Message me before ordering if your project is custom.”

    What gets gigs flagged

    Fiverr doesn't allow contact details or ways to move the conversation off the platform. The checker warns you about emails, links, phone numbers and mentions of outside apps, so you can remove them before publishing. Pair this with a correctly sized gallery image from the gig image resizer.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the character limit for a Fiverr gig title?

    80 characters, including the “I will” that every title starts with.

    How long can a Fiverr gig description be?

    Up to 1,200 characters, with a minimum of 120. Most strong descriptions use 800–1,200.

    How many search tags can I add to a Fiverr gig?

    Up to 5. Use the phrases buyers actually search for, and keep each one short.

    What is the Fiverr profile description limit?

    Between 150 and 600 characters.

    Is my text saved?

    Yes, in your browser on this device only, so you can come back to a draft. Use “Clear all” to remove it.

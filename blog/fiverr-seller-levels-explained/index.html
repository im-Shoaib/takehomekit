Fiverr seller levels explained

Levels bring more gig slots, more trust and, at the top, faster access to your money. Here's what each level asks for and what actually moves the numbers.

Key points
  • Fiverr has three levels above new seller: Level 1, Level 2 and Top Rated. Top Rated also needs a manual review.
  • Each level has minimums for success score, rating, response rate, completed orders, unique buyers and earnings.
  • The success score reflects buyer satisfaction and order outcomes — not just your star rating.
  • Fiverr can change thresholds. Your Levels page in Fiverr shows exactly where you stand.

The levels at a glance

These are the requirements Fiverr's help centre lists at the time of writing. Fiverr updates its level system from time to time, so treat your own Levels dashboard as the final word.

What the success score is

The success score is Fiverr's measure of how well your orders go, on a scale up to 10. It looks beyond star ratings at things like buyer satisfaction, communication, order cancellations and how orders conclude. A seller with a 4.9 rating but frequent cancellations can still have a low score.

What each level gets you

  • More active gigs and gig extras, so you can cover more services and raise order values.
  • More trust in search results — a badge buyers recognise.
  • Faster access to earnings at the top. Top Rated sellers' earnings clear in 7 days instead of the usual 14.
  • Better support at higher levels.

How to level up faster

Protect your success score

  • Clarify requirements before you start. Most cancellations and bad reviews come from mismatched expectations.
  • Don't accept orders you can't deliver well. Politely decline or suggest a custom offer instead.
  • Send updates on longer orders so buyers never wonder what's happening.
  • Resolve issues in the conversation before they become disputes.

Hit the order and buyer counts

  • Offer a small, fast Basic package that new buyers can say yes to easily.
  • Unique buyers matter: repeat orders from one client help your earnings but count as one buyer.

Keep your response rate high

  • Reply to every new message within 24 hours — even a short “thanks, I'll review this today” counts.
  • Use the Fiverr mobile app so messages don't wait until you're at your desk.

Grow earnings, not just order count

  • Use Standard and Premium packages and gig extras to raise the average order.
  • Write gigs buyers can find — see our gig SEO guide.

If you drop a level

Levels are reviewed regularly, and falling below a requirement can move you down. Don't panic: look at which metric slipped on your Levels page, fix the cause (often response time or a run of cancellations), and focus on a few smooth orders.

Requirements as listed in Fiverr's help centre article “Understanding Fiverr's freelancer levels” at the time of writing. Fiverr may change them — check your Levels page.

Frequently asked questions

How many orders do I need for Level 1 on Fiverr?

Fiverr's help centre lists 5 completed orders from at least 3 unique buyers and $400 in earnings, along with a success score of 5+, a 4.4+ rating and an 80% response rate.

How do I become Top Rated on Fiverr?

Meet the Top Rated thresholds — including a 9+ success score, 40 orders, 20 unique buyers and $10,000 in earnings — and pass Fiverr's manual review.

Do higher levels pay lower Fiverr fees?

No. The 20% seller fee is the same for every level.

What is the Fiverr success score?

A score up to 10 reflecting buyer satisfaction and how your orders conclude, including cancellations — not just your star rating.

Fees, limits and rules change. We check them regularly and date every update, but always confirm the final number with the platform, your provider or an adviser before you rely on it.

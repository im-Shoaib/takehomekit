Upwork Connects explained: what they cost and how to spend them

Every Upwork proposal costs Connects, and Connects cost money. Treat them like an ad budget and you'll win more work for less.

Key points
  • Connects cost $0.15 each. The number a proposal needs is shown on each job before you apply.
  • Boosted proposals let you bid extra Connects for a higher spot in the client's list.
  • Connects are refunded in limited cases, such as when a client closes a job without hiring.
  • Track your cost per job won, not cost per proposal.

What Connects are

Connects are Upwork's tokens for applying to jobs. Each proposal you send costs a number of Connects, which Upwork shows on the job post before you apply. You can buy more at $0.15 each, and some plans include a monthly allowance.

That makes every proposal a small investment. A proposal that costs 16 Connects is $2.40 — trivial once, but real when you send dozens a week.

How many Connects a proposal costs

The cost varies by job. Upwork sets it per posting, taking into account things like the job's size and type. Always check the number before writing your proposal, and ask yourself whether this job is worth that spend.

Boosted proposals

When you apply, you can choose to boost your proposal by bidding extra Connects. The highest bids are shown at the top of the client's proposal list. If you're outbid, you only spend the Connects for a normal proposal.

  • Boost selectively — on jobs that are a strong fit and worth enough to justify the extra cost.
  • Boosting doesn't fix a weak proposal. The first two lines still decide whether the client opens it.

Free and included Connects

Upwork gives freelancers some Connects without buying them — for example a monthly allowance on the basic plan, extra Connects with paid membership plans and as rewards for strong profiles. Amounts and plan prices change, so check the Membership & Connects page in your account for your current allowance.

When Connects are refunded

Connects are generally returned if the client closes the job without hiring anyone, or if Upwork removes the job. They're not returned just because another freelancer was hired. Applying to a job you were invited to doesn't cost Connects.

The number that matters: cost per job won

Instead of worrying about each proposal, measure what it costs you to win a job:

Cost per win = (Connects spent ÷ jobs won) × $0.15

If you spend 160 Connects to win one job, that job cost you $24 in Connects — on top of Upwork's service fee. Include both when you decide what to charge.

Seven ways to spend fewer Connects per win

  1. Apply early. Jobs posted in the last hour or two have fewer proposals competing.
  2. Read the client's history. Payment verified, past hires and fair reviews mean a real chance of hiring.
  3. Skip vague posts. One-line briefs with no budget rarely lead to hires.
  4. Answer the client's questions in your first lines.
  5. Specialise. Fewer, better-matched proposals win more often.
  6. Boost only when it's worth it.
  7. Reuse structure, not text. A proven proposal structure saves time without sounding copy-pasted.

Frequently asked questions

How much do Upwork Connects cost?

$0.15 each when you buy them. Some membership plans include a monthly allowance.

How many Connects does a proposal cost?

It varies by job. Upwork shows the number on each job post before you apply.

Do I get my Connects back if I'm not hired?

Generally only if the client closes the job without hiring anyone or Upwork removes the job — not when another freelancer is hired.

Is boosting a proposal worth it?

Sometimes. Boost on high-value jobs that fit you well; for most jobs, a strong early proposal matters more.

Fees, limits and rules change. We check them regularly and date every update, but always confirm the final number with the platform, your provider or an adviser before you rely on it.

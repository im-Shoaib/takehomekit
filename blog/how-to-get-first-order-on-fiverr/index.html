How to get your first order on Fiverr

The first order is the hardest one. Once you have a review, buyers trust you more and Fiverr shows your gig more. Here's how to get that first one without gaming the system.

Key points
  • Go narrow: a specific gig beats a general one when you have no reviews.
  • Write the title around words buyers actually search for.
  • Price to win the first few orders — but never below your floor after Fiverr's 20%.
  • Reply fast, deliver early, and over-communicate. Your first review is your best marketing.

Why the first order is so hard

Buyers on Fiverr choose between dozens of gigs that look similar. With no reviews, you have no proof — so everything else on your gig has to work harder: the title, the image, the price, the packages and how quickly you reply. The steps below fix each one.

1. Pick a narrow service

“I will do web development” competes with thousands of established sellers. “I will fix WordPress speed issues and pass Core Web Vitals” competes with far fewer — and buyers searching for that exact problem find you.

  • Choose one problem you can solve well and quickly.
  • Search it on Fiverr. If the first page is full of sellers with thousands of reviews, go one step narrower.
  • Create one gig per service rather than one gig for everything.

2. Write a title and description buyers search for

Type your service into Fiverr's search bar and note the suggestions — they come from real searches. Put the main phrase early in your title, within the 80-character limit, and use the 1,200-character description to explain exactly what's included. Our gig SEO guide covers this step by step.

3. Make the gig image do the selling

Your image is the first thing buyers see in search. Show the result — a finished site, a before-and-after, a mock-up — at Fiverr's recommended 1280 × 769 px, with a few large words at most.

4. Price to win — without working for free

A slightly lower starting price helps you win the first few orders. But remember Fiverr keeps 20%: a $10 gig pays you $8. Work out the lowest price that still pays you fairly, start there, and raise prices after five to ten good reviews.

  • Make Basic a small, fast job — easy to say yes to.
  • Make Standard your real offer.
  • Add gig extras (faster delivery, extra revision) to lift the order value.

5. Complete your profile

Buyers check your profile before ordering a new seller. Use a clear photo of yourself, list your skills, and write a short description that says what you do, who you help and one result you've achieved.

6. Reply fast and be available

New sellers win orders in the inbox. Reply to messages quickly, ask one or two smart questions about the buyer's project, and send a custom offer when the request doesn't fit your packages. Keep the Fiverr app on your phone so you see messages promptly.

7. Share your gig outside Fiverr

Share your gig link on your LinkedIn, in relevant groups where self-promotion is allowed, and with past clients or contacts. One real buyer from outside Fiverr can give you the first review that unlocks the rest.

8. Deliver an outstanding first order

  • Confirm the requirements in writing before you start.
  • Send a short progress update.
  • Deliver early if you can, and explain what you did.
  • Politely ask the buyer to leave a review if they're happy.

What not to do

  • Don't buy fake reviews or orders. It breaks Fiverr's rules and can get your account removed.
  • Don't share contact details or ask to move the conversation off Fiverr.
  • Don't copy other sellers' descriptions or images.
  • Don't keep changing your gig every day. Give changes a week or two to show results.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get the first order on Fiverr?

It varies widely by niche and gig quality. A narrow gig, a strong image and fast replies shorten the wait more than anything else.

Should I lower my price to get the first order?

A slightly lower starting price helps, but stay above your minimum after Fiverr's 20% fee and raise prices once you have reviews.

Can I share my Fiverr gig on social media?

Yes. Sharing your gig link is allowed and can bring your first buyers. Just don't ask buyers to pay you outside Fiverr.

Fees, limits and rules change. We check them regularly and date every update, but always confirm the final number with the platform, your provider or an adviser before you rely on it.

How to withdraw money from Fiverr in Pakistan

From a completed order to rupees in your bank account — every step, every fee, and the small habits that save you thousands of rupees a year.

Key points
  • In Pakistan, Fiverr pays out through Payoneer. Even the “bank transfer” option runs through Payoneer.
  • Fiverr charges a flat $3 per withdrawal, not per order — so withdrawing in batches saves money.
  • Payoneer converts dollars to rupees at a rate up to about 2% below mid-market.
  • Keep the bank's Proof of Remittance Certificate (PRC) — it supports the low tax rate on IT exports.

How the money moves

Before the steps, it helps to see the whole journey. Your money passes through four stages, and each one has its own wait or fee:

  1. Order completed. The buyer accepts your delivery (or it auto-completes). Fiverr keeps its 20% service fee.
  2. Pending clearance. Earnings are held for 14 days — 7 days for Top Rated sellers and Fiverr Pro — before you can withdraw them.
  3. Fiverr to Payoneer. You withdraw your available balance to Payoneer. Fiverr charges a flat fee per withdrawal.
  4. Payoneer to your bank. Payoneer converts the dollars to rupees and sends them to your local bank account, usually within one to three business days.

Step 1: Open and verify a Payoneer account

If you don't have one yet, sign up for Payoneer — you can start from Fiverr's withdrawal page or from Payoneer's website. A few things save headaches later:

  • Use your real name exactly as it appears on your CNIC. It must match your Fiverr account and your bank account title.
  • Complete identity verification straight away. Accounts under review can't withdraw.
  • Use an email and phone number you'll keep long-term. Payoneer sends verification codes to both.

Step 2: Add Payoneer as your Fiverr withdrawal method

  1. On Fiverr, go to Earnings (from your profile menu, under Billing / Earnings).
  2. Choose Payoneer as your withdrawal method and sign in to Payoneer when asked to link the accounts.
  3. Wait for the link to be confirmed. After you add or change a Payoneer method, Fiverr makes you wait 24 hours before your first withdrawal.

Step 3: Withdraw from Fiverr to Payoneer

  1. In Earnings, check your Available for withdrawal balance. Pending earnings can't be withdrawn yet.
  2. Click Withdraw, choose Payoneer and enter the amount.
  3. Confirm through the email or two-factor prompt Fiverr sends you.

Fiverr deducts a flat $3 from each withdrawal. It's the same whether you withdraw $50 or $1,000, which is why the timing of your withdrawals matters (more on that below).

Step 4: Withdraw from Payoneer to your bank in PKR

  1. In Payoneer, add your Pakistani bank account: bank name, IBAN and an account title that matches your Payoneer name.
  2. Go to Withdraw → To bank account, pick the bank and choose the amount.
  3. Payoneer shows the exchange rate and the rupee amount before you confirm. Check it.
  4. Confirm. Funds usually arrive in one to three business days.

Five ways to keep more of every withdrawal

  • Withdraw in batches. Five $100 withdrawals cost $15 in Fiverr fees; one $500 withdrawal costs $3.
  • Check the rate before you confirm. Payoneer shows it on the confirmation screen. If you're not in a hurry, compare it with the day's mid-market rate.
  • Keep every PRC. Ask your bank for the Proof of Remittance Certificate for each transfer. It documents your income as an IT export, which is what lets you pay the reduced final tax.
  • Register with PSEB. It cuts the tax on your remittances from 1% to 0.25%. Our PSEB guide walks through it.
  • Watch Payoneer's annual fee. Payoneer may charge an account fee if you receive less than a set amount in a year. Check the Fees page in your account.

Common problems and fixes

“Withdraw” button is greyed out

Your earnings are probably still pending clearance, or you changed your withdrawal method less than 24 hours ago.

Payoneer withdrawal rejected by the bank

Almost always a name mismatch. The bank account title, Payoneer name and CNIC name must match. Fix it with your bank or Payoneer support.

Money left Payoneer but hasn't arrived

Allow three business days, then contact your bank with the Payoneer transaction details. Weekends and bank holidays add delays.

Frequently asked questions

Can I withdraw from Fiverr directly to a Pakistani bank?

In Pakistan, Fiverr's payouts go through Payoneer. You withdraw from Fiverr to Payoneer, then from Payoneer to your local bank in PKR.

How much does Fiverr charge to withdraw to Payoneer?

A flat $3 per withdrawal, regardless of the amount. If your withdrawal screen shows a different fee, that's the one that applies to your account.

How long does Fiverr take to release my money?

Earnings clear 14 days after an order completes, or 7 days for Top Rated sellers and Fiverr Pro. After that, Payoneer to your bank usually takes one to three business days.

Do I pay tax on Fiverr income in Pakistan?

Yes. IT and IT-enabled service exports can be taxed at a reduced final rate on remittances — 0.25% if you're registered with PSEB, or 1% if you're not — provided you file your return.

Fees, limits and rules change. We check them regularly and date every update, but always confirm the final number with the platform, your provider or an adviser before you rely on it.

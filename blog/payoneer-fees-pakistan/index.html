Payoneer fees in Pakistan explained

Payoneer rarely shows one big fee. Instead, small charges appear at different steps. Here's each one, what it costs on a real withdrawal, and how to pay less.

Key points
  • Payoneer charges at three points: when money arrives, when it's converted to PKR, and once a year if your account is low-volume.
  • Withdrawing to a PKR bank account costs a conversion fee — Payoneer's pricing lists a range, and around 2% is typical. You see the exact rate before you confirm.
  • Since February 2026, the $29.95 annual fee applies if your account receives less than $6,000 in 12 months (where the rule applies).
  • Fiverr's own $3 per withdrawal is separate and charged before the money reaches Payoneer.

1. Fees for receiving money

From Fiverr, Upwork and other marketplaces

When a marketplace pays you, the marketplace usually takes its own withdrawal fee first. Fiverr, for example, deducts a flat $3 per withdrawal before the money lands in Payoneer — whether you withdraw $50 or $1,000. Payoneer says its side of marketplace payouts varies by marketplace, so check the fee shown on your Payoneer transaction.

From direct clients

If a client pays you through a Payoneer payment request, the fee depends on how they pay:

  • Bank transfer or US ACH: 1%, with a $1 minimum. A $500 payment costs you $5.
  • Credit or debit card: up to 3.99% + $0.49. The same $500 could cost about $20.44.

For direct clients, asking for a bank transfer instead of a card payment is the easiest saving in this whole article.

2. The conversion fee when you withdraw to PKR

This is the fee most freelancers never notice, because it isn't shown as a separate line. When you withdraw dollars to a Pakistani bank account in rupees, Payoneer converts them at its own rate, which is below the mid-market rate you see on Google. The difference is the conversion fee.

Payoneer's public pricing lists a range for withdrawals that involve conversion, and around 2% is the commonly quoted figure for Pakistan. The good news: Payoneer shows the exact rate and rupee amount on the confirmation screen before you withdraw.

Rupees received ≈ dollars withdrawn × mid-market rate × (1 − conversion fee)

Withdrawing in dollars instead

Some freelancers keep a USD account at a Pakistani bank to avoid Payoneer's conversion. Check the fee first: in 2025, Pakistani users reported a 3% charge on withdrawals to non-PKR (foreign-currency) accounts and to third-party accounts. Payoneer's public pages don't list this separately, so confirm it on your account's Fees page — and remember your bank will apply its own rate when you later convert to rupees.

3. The annual account fee

Payoneer charges a $29.95 annual account fee to low-volume accounts. From 23 February 2026, in the countries where the new rule applies, the fee is charged if your account receives less than $6,000 in 12 months (the previous threshold was much lower). In other countries it applies only if the account has no transactions for 12 months.

If you're just starting out and receive a few hundred dollars a year, $29.95 can be a large share of your earnings — factor it in when you compare payout options.

4. Payoneer-to-Payoneer transfers

Receiving money from another Payoneer user is free. Sending can cost up to $4 within the same country, and up to 1% plus up to $4 to another country, according to Payoneer's pricing page. Pakistani users reported extra charges on these transfers in 2025, so check the amount shown before you send.

Six ways to pay Payoneer less

  • Withdraw in larger, less frequent amounts. Fixed fees like Fiverr's $3 shrink as a share of bigger withdrawals.
  • Check the rate on the confirmation screen. If you're not in a hurry and the rate looks poor, wait a day.
  • Ask direct clients for bank transfers, not cards. 1% versus up to 3.99% + $0.49.
  • Know whether the annual fee applies to you. If you receive less than $6,000 a year, include $29.95 in your costs.
  • Compare routes once in a while. Fees change; a quick comparison tells you whether Payoneer is still your cheapest option.
  • Read the Fees page in your account. It's updated before changes take effect and is the only figure that's guaranteed for your account.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Payoneer charge to withdraw to a Pakistani bank?

Withdrawing in PKR costs a currency conversion fee built into the exchange rate — around 2% is typical. Payoneer shows the exact rate and rupee amount before you confirm.

What is Payoneer's annual fee in 2026?

$29.95. Since 23 February 2026, in countries where the new rule applies, it's charged if your account receives less than $6,000 in 12 months.

Does Payoneer charge for receiving money from Fiverr?

Fiverr deducts its own $3 per withdrawal. Payoneer says its fee for marketplace payouts varies by marketplace, so check the transaction details in your account.

Is it cheaper to withdraw in USD?

Not always. Pakistani users reported a 3% fee on withdrawals to foreign-currency accounts in 2025, and your bank applies its own rate when you convert. Compare both before switching.

Fees, limits and rules change. We check them regularly and date every update, but always confirm the final number with the platform, your provider or an adviser before you rely on it.
